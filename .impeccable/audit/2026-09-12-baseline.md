## Implementation Integrity Verdict

Pass. The page expresses one product-specific system: a Roman stone/oxblood world and an AI ink/gold world. Both are carried by named tokens (`pretor.css:35-66`, retuned at `1154-1172`). The claims in the markup are annotated against the code that backs them. Nothing reads as an interchangeable template.

The drift is real but bounded. It comes from stacking a hand-written layer over compiled output instead of editing the output:
- Nine selectors in section 1 have no markup.
- Six `.sec__check` spans are rendered and then hidden.
- `.btn--primary` is defined three times.
- One live declaration still uses the retired oxblood.
- Two code comments describe intro behaviour the code does not have (measured, see P3-4).

## Executive Summary

- Audit Health Score: 11/20 (Acceptable).
- Issues: P0 0, P1 5, P2 7, P3 6.
- Top issues:
  1. Focus indicators fail 1.4.11 on every light section and on the leader agent card.
  2. The hero lines "Nada se entrega sin su aprobación." and "Pretor es para abogados matriculados..." render at 2.04:1.
  3. The intro stage is `aria-hidden` but takes the first Tab stop and covers the page skip link.
  4. 200% text breaks the desktop layout (1.4.4).
  5. The intro costs 714 KB and blocks the hero behind 1680px of scrolling on desktop and about 4.2 s of overlay on phones.
- Next steps: fix the focus outline and the hero gold text first (both are one-rule changes). Then make the intro stage inert to AT and keyboard, and add `scroll-padding-top`. Then handle zoom and the intro payload.

## Detailed Findings by Severity

### P1 Major

#### [P1-1] Focus outline below 3:1 on light backgrounds
- Location: `pretor.css:1755-1761` (`a, button, summary :focus-visible { outline: 2px solid var(--gold) }`) overrides the section-aware rule at `pretor.css:280-287` for every link, button and summary. Chips on the leader card use the global oxblood outline on ink (`index.html:534-549`).
- Category: Accessibility
- Evidence (`focus.mjs`, landing 1440). 71 tab stops; every stop has a visible outline (0 without one); 26 are below 3:1:
  - `#cda455` on `#f4f0e8`, 2.04:1, 4 stops: skip link, "Crear su estudio", WhatsApp ghost button, Cedap link.
  - `#cda455` on `#ece6d8`, 1.87:1, 8 stops: "Cuéntenos qué necesita", guide link, channel links, founder links.
  - `#cda455` on `#fbf8f1`, 2.19:1, 11 stops: "Regístrese acá" and all 10 FAQ summaries.
  - `#6e2233` on `#1a1a1a`, 1.61:1, 3 stops: the P/U/X chips on the leader card.
  - Legal pages: 8 of 14 stops (privacidad) and 9 of 13 (terminos) at 2.04:1, including the skip link.
- Impact: keyboard users lose track of focus in the hero, FAQ, founder and legal prose.
- WCAG: 1.4.11 Non-text Contrast (AA), 2.4.7 Focus Visible.
- Recommendation: scope the gold outline to `.section--dark` and the nav. Use oxblood (9.5:1 on stone) elsewhere. On `.agent--leader`, use gold.
- Suggested command: /impeccable harden

#### [P1-2] Gold hero text on stone at 2.04:1
- Location: `index.html:213`, `index.html:249` (`.hero__flow-foot`, color `pretor.css:2248`); the step counters `.hero__flow-steps li::before` (`pretor.css:2238`).
- Category: Accessibility
- Evidence (`contrast.mjs`): `#cda455` on `#f4f0e8` is 2.04:1 at 14.08px/400 (1440) and 12.56px (400). The counters are 10.98px/9.8px. Detector agrees (2.0:1).
- Impact: two of the hero's key lines, including the eligibility statement, are hard to read in daylight.
- WCAG: 1.4.3 Contrast Minimum (AA).
- Recommendation: use `var(--oxblood)` on light sections. Keep gold only under `.section--dark`.
- Suggested command: /impeccable colorize

#### [P1-3] Intro stage is aria-hidden but focusable, and covers the skip link
- Location: `pretor-intro.js:76` (`aria-hidden="true"`), `pretor-intro.js:86` (skip `<button>` inside it). Stacking: `.prologo` `z-index: 150` (`pretor.css:2878`) against `.skip-link` `z-index: 100` (`pretor.css:508`).
- Category: Accessibility
- Evidence (`intro.mjs`, `verify.mjs`, 1440, motion on):
  - First Tab focuses `button.prologo__saltar` with `inAriaHidden: true`. A screen reader announces nothing.
  - Second Tab focuses the page skip link at rect `[16,16,180,47]`. `elementFromPoint` at its centre returns `prologo__carga`, so it is fully obscured.
  - On phones the fixed overlay has the same 150 over 100 stacking.
- Impact: screen-reader users land on an unannounced control. Sighted keyboard users cannot see the skip link while the stage is up.
- WCAG: 4.1.2 Name, Role, Value (aria-hidden focusable content), 2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured (AA).
- Recommendation: drop `aria-hidden` from the stage and label the skip button. Or make the stage `inert` and move the skip control out of it. Raise `.skip-link` above 150.
- Suggested command: /impeccable harden

#### [P1-4] 200% text breaks the desktop layout
- Location: nav row `pretor.css:302-307` with the px breakpoint `418-425`. `overflow: hidden` clips on `.marco__grid` `640`, `.agents__grid` `729`, `.sec__promises` `821`, `.sec__layers` `970`, `.cap__card` `1305`, `.demo__chat` `1473`. `.sec__table-wrap` has no `overflow-x` (`893-895`), unlike `.versus__wrap` (`2320`) and `.legal__table-wrap` (`2807`).
- Category: Responsive

Agent cards cut words mid-line (`zoom200-index-1440-agentes.png`). Trifecta `td.sec__why` cells end at x=1586 to 1602.

  - Add `overflow-wrap: anywhere` or `hyphens: auto` to `.display`.
- Suggested command: /impeccable adapt

#### [P1-5] Intro payload and blocking cost
- Location: `pretor-intro.js:4-15` (gates), `42-68` (mobile timer), `143-170` (desktop scroll progress), `175` (preload list); `pretor.css:2873-2876` (`height: 300vh`), `3053-3062` (mobile overlay `pointer-events: auto`).
- Category: Performance
- Evidence (`intro.mjs`, `verify.mjs`, `verify2.mjs`):
  - Bytes: `world.webp` 372,916 (2560x1299), `curtain-r.webp` 133 KB (1392x1536), `curtain-l.webp` 117 KB (1360x1536), `dust.webp` 91 KB (2048x1152). Desktop fetches all four (714 KB). A 400px phone fetches three (623 KB), including the 2560px world. This happens on every non-reduced load; there is no per-session check.
  - For comparison, the whole landing page without the intro is 333,731 bytes on first load (HTML 101,774; CSS 80,163; fonts 105,660; justitia.png 38,090; intro JS 7,447). Scrolling lazy-loads another 79,097 bytes of images.
  - Desktop, no interaction: the stage is still present after 8 s. The hero title sits at y=2015.
  - Desktop, reaching the hero: 1680px of wheel scrolling (15 steps of 120px), after which the page jumps back to y=0. Escape or the skip button also end it.
  - Mobile: the overlay covers the viewport centre and the `h1` from 162 ms to 4391 ms (36 of 42 samples). A tap dismisses it within 800 ms.
  - Slow link (1.6 Mbps, 150 ms latency): desktop injected the black loader at 1882 ms and removed it at 4437 ms, when the 2.5 s preload budget expired. The hero became visible about 4.4 s after navigation, against an LCP of 1820 ms for the same profile with reduced motion. The LCP API reported the loader span (1888 ms), so field LCP will under-report this delay.
  - Slow link, mobile: the 2.2 s budget expired and the intro never showed, but all three images were still downloaded.
- Impact: first-visit mobile data cost triples. Returning visitors pay it again. On slow links the hero is delayed by about 2.6 s.
- Standard: Core Web Vitals (LCP as experienced), Save-Data etiquette. CLAUDE.md records the replay as deliberate; the cost is reported, not the decision.
- Recommendation:
  - Serve a phone-sized world (about 900px wide) through `image-set()` or a media query.
  - Abort the fetches when the budget expires.
  - Consider a per-session flag, which the file header already claims exists.
  - Cap the desktop stage at less than 200vh of scroll.
- Suggested command: /impeccable optimize

## Patterns & Systemic Issues

- Gold is used as if every surface were dark. The same token drives the focus outline (P1-1), hero text (P1-2) and step counters on stone, where it cannot reach 3:1. The section-aware rule at `pretor.css:285-287` was right; `1755-1761` flattened it.
- Overrides stack instead of replacing. Section 2 re-declares tokens, buttons, nav and cards over section 1, which leaves dead rules and stale literals (P2-7, P3-4). Every future edit has to check three places.
- `overflow: hidden` is used as a rounding tool on text containers (six grids). It is harmless at 100% text and destructive at 200% (P1-4).
- The intro owns the first seconds and the first Tab stop, and its gating comments do not match its behaviour (P1-3, P1-5, P3-4).
- Small mono labels carry meaning (P2-4, P2-5, P2-6). The trifecta access state is at once the smallest text, the smallest touch target and the least accessible control on the page.

## Positive Findings

- Horizontal overflow at 100% text: none on any page at 320, 400 or 768px (`scrollWidth == clientWidth`), and none on the landing page at 900 to 1280 with every `details` open. Wide tables scroll inside their own box on the legal pages (`legal__table-wrap` 544/278 at 320).
- Contrast is otherwise strong. The landing page renders 56 distinct effective text/background colour pairs; outside the failures above, the lowest is 5.08:1. The legal pages render 17 and 14 distinct groups with 0 failures and none below 5.5:1. There are no form fields, so no placeholders, on any page.
- Every one of the 71 landing tab stops has a visible outline, and `:focus-visible` matches on each. No element is left without a focus state.
- Skip link: first in `body` on all three pages; visible at y=16 when focused; Enter then Tab lands inside `main` ("Crear su estudio" on the landing page, the first mail link on legal pages).
- Headings: one `h1` per page and no skipped levels. The landing page runs h1, h2, h3, with h4 only inside the security `details`. Legal pages run h1 then h2.
- Landmarks: banner, navigation "Principal" (desktop), main and contentinfo on the landing page. Named regions for the two quote bands and both tables. No duplicate ids.
- `details`/`summary`: Chrome exposes DisclosureTriangle with `expanded` state; Enter opens and Space closes. The chevrons are `aria-hidden`.
- Images: every `img` has `width` and `height`. Every below-fold image is `loading="lazy"` except `.hero__mark`, which reuses the nav mark's URL and costs no extra bytes. Alt text is meaningful for the QR codes and portraits and empty for decorative marks.
- Reduced motion is handled deliberately, not only by the global kill. With `reduce`: 74 of 74 below-fold `.reveal` elements are opaque with `transform: none`, 0 demo children hidden, typing indicator `display: none`, `.hero__mark` `animation-name: none`, `scroll-behavior: auto`, no intro.

- Console: no page errors, no 4xx, no JS exceptions on any page in either motion mode. The only error is the Cloudflare beacon, which the harness blocked.
- Self-hosted variable fonts with `font-display: swap` and matching preloads.
