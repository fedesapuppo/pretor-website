## 1. Design specificity verdict

Partly authored. The content and a few set pieces belong to this product. The composition around them is a category template.

Specific to Pretor:
- The `#demo` chat. It shows an escrito with cited articles, the agent hand-offs and an approval gate, in the channel the lawyer already uses. No other product could use it unchanged.
- The trifecta table, the versus rows and the per-agent access chips. Each one names a real mechanism.
- The hero flow block (audio of a meeting to a signed escrito).
- The Roman-law diptych, Justitia emblem and Latin maxims. This is a brand world, though it works at costume depth (see below).

Category-interchangeable:
- The same section head repeats about 14 times: eyebrow, display heading, grey lead.
- A dark SaaS feature grid of 12 same-size cards numbered 01 to 12 (`.cap__card`).
- Pricing cards on black with a glowing featured tier.
- Film grain, radial gold and oxblood glows, gold hairline seams between bands.
- Code-comment eyebrows (`// Seguridad`) and mono captions on the dark bands. This is the "premium AI startup" kit.
- Four separate anonymous testimonial treatments.

The Roman layer is applied as typeface and color (Cinzel, gold, marble). The structure under it is a generic long-scroll SaaS page. The page also alternates two identities band by band. The light bands use Cinzel caps, oxblood and a tracked eyebrow with a rule. The dark bands use Inter, gold and `//` mono. The stylesheet declares this on purpose ("Keeps both identities", pretor.css:1148). The effect is two sites stitched together, not one authored world. A black-and-white direction removes the color, so it forces a choice between those two type systems.

## 3. Cognitive load

Checklist:
- [ ] Single focus: fail. The hero stacks an h1, a bordered four-step block, a lead, a mono paragraph, two CTAs and a qualifier line. The closing section offers WhatsApp, not the sign-up.
- [ ] Chunking (≤4 per group): fail. 12 capability cards, 10 agents, 9 versus rows, 6 security promises, 10 FAQ items.
- [x] Grouping: pass. The 1px hairline grids and the card panels group related items well.
- [x] Visual hierarchy: pass with a caveat. Each section has a clear head. The standalone testimonial band sits right after the hero at near-display size and competes with the h1.
- [x] One thing at a time: pass. The plan decision is 4 cards, with the top-ups placed after.
- [ ] Minimal choices (≤4): fail. The nav has 8 items on both desktop and the mobile panel (6 anchors, Ingresar, Registrarse).
- [ ] Working memory: fail. The P/U/X chip meaning must be carried from a tooltip or from a later section.
- [x] Progressive disclosure: pass. Trifecta `<details>`, FAQ accordions.

Result: 4 failures, so cognitive load is high.

Decision points with more than 4 visible options: nav (8), `.cap__grid` (12), `.agents__grid` (10), FAQ (10), versus table (9 rows). The plan area shows 4 plans, 2 reload packs, a promo link and a source link in one view.

## 4. Emotional journey

1. Entry (desktop, motion allowed): a curtain parts over a forum and "LEX ANTIQVA MENS NOVA" appears. This is theatre and awe. For a busy lawyer on a second visit it is a toll, because it replays on every load.
2. Hero: gravitas from Cinzel. The four-step flow is the first real peak of interest because it is concrete. The CTA arrives only after about 1,150px on desktop and 1,180px on a 400px phone, below the first viewport on both.
3. Diptych, proof and testimonial: brand statement, then reassurance. Every quote is anonymous ("un usuario profesional del derecho", five times), so the reassurance is thin for an audience that trusts names and matrículas.
4. Marco and 12 capabilities: an overwhelm valley. The "Pará, pará, pará" quote lands as relief, because it voices the reader's own overload.
5. Agents: a complexity valley. Ten cards with unexplained colored chips.
6. Demo: the strongest peak. It is legible and specific, and it ends in an approval button.
7. Versus: confidence on desktop. On mobile the row labels break one word per line, so the key argument turns into a visual fault.
8. Security: a reassurance moment at the highest-stakes topic (secreto profesional), handled well with the deeper layer collapsed.
9. Por qué: repetition fatigue. It restates earlier sections.
10. Planes: high stakes. Prices are clear. The reassurance lines (balance never expires, warning before top-up) are set at step--1 in `--dmuted`, so the calming facts are whispered.
11. Founders: a humanizing peak with faces and names.
12. End: gold emblem, "Lleve su estudio al estándar de Pretor", a WhatsApp button and a personal email. Peak-end rule: the last action offered is a chat with the founder, not the self-serve sign-up the hero sells. The end is soft.

## 6. Priority issues

### [P1] The versus table breaks on phones: row labels stack one word per line
- Why: `#diferencia` carries the core claim ("No es un chat que sabe de derecho"). At 400px every row header renders as a column of single Cinzel words ("Qué / día / es / hoy", idx-m-06 to idx-m-08). Most of this audience reads on a phone. The section looks broken exactly where it should look rigorous.
- Root cause: `.versus__table tbody th { width: 22% }` (pretor.css:2350-2354, specificity 0,1,2). It beats the mobile reset `.versus__table th, .versus__table td { width: auto }` inside `@media (max-width:700px)` (pretor.css:2374-2380, specificity 0,1,1). The header stays 22% of the card, about 75px.
- Fix: inside the 700px media block add `.versus__table tbody th { width: auto; }`. While there, set the mobile row header in the body face at `--step-0`, weight 600 (pretor.css:2381-2385 currently switches it to Cinzel `--step-1`). An all-caps display face in a narrow stacked label is the second half of the problem.
- Suggested command: /impeccable adapt

### [P1] Gold text and the focus ring fail contrast on every light section
- Why: `.hero__flow-foot` is `#cda455` on `#f4f0e8` at 2.04:1 (pretor.css:2244-2249). It sets two load-bearing lines: "Nada se entrega sin su aprobación" and the audience qualifier under the CTAs (index.html:213, 249-251). The hero step numerals use the same gold (pretor.css:2238). Subsection 20 also overrides the oxblood ring from section 1 with a gold ring on every `a`, `button` and `summary` (pretor.css:1755-1761). On cream that ring is 2.04:1, below the 3:1 floor for non-text contrast (WCAG 1.4.11), so keyboard users lose their place on most of the page.
- Fix: on light grounds use `color: var(--ink)` or `var(--oxblood)` (9.5:1) for `.hero__flow-foot` and `.hero__flow-steps li::before`. Scope the gold ring to `.section--dark a:focus-visible, .section--dark button:focus-visible, .section--dark summary:focus-visible`. Let light sections keep `outline: 2px solid var(--ink)`. In the black-and-white direction this becomes a single `outline: 2px solid currentColor; outline-offset: 3px`.
- Suggested command: /impeccable audit

### [P1] The P/U/X chips carry meaning by hue alone, are explained by hover only, and are defined two sections later
- Why:
  - The chips (pretor.css:2094-2120, index.html:530-584) encode exposure as red (Sí), green (No) and amber (partial).
  - The three text colors have almost the same luminance: 0.089, 0.111 and 0.117. Sí against No is 1.16:1 and No against partial is 1.04:1. In grayscale, or for a color-blind reader, all 27 chips look alike, and the reader must read each label.
  - The polarity also inverts the usual reading. Green means the agent has no access, and red "Sí" means exposure. A lawyer can read "Sí" in red as a failure of the agent.
  - The definitions sit in `data-tip` pseudo-element tooltips that open on `:hover` or `:focus-visible` (pretor.css:2152-2182). On touch that is unreliable.
  - The only visible legend is inside the collapsed trifecta `<details>` in `#seguridad`, two sections later. That is a memory bridge.
- Fix:
  - Encode by shape and fill, not hue. "Sí" (has access): solid ink fill, stone text. "No": 1px outline, ink text, transparent fill. Partial: 1px dashed outline.
  - Use the same three treatments for `.leg--yes/--no/--partial` in the dark trifecta table (pretor.css:951-962), inverted.
  - Place one static legend row above `.agents__grid`. Reuse the three definitions already written in `data-tip`, not new wording.
  - Delete the tooltip rules and the `tabindex="0"` on the 27 chips. They add 27 tab stops that do nothing without hover.
- Suggested command: /impeccable clarify

### [P1] The primary action sits below the first screen and is missing at the end
- Why:
  - "Crear su estudio" is at about 1,150px on a 1440 desktop and about 1,180px on a 400px phone. The flow block, the lead and a mono paragraph all come first (index.html:194-248). On desktop the hero column is capped at 980px and left-aligned, so the right 40% of the first screen at 1440 is empty cream while the CTA falls below the fold.
  - The page ends in `#contacto` with WhatsApp as the only button (index.html:1818-1845). The self-serve sign-up the hero sells is never repeated after `#planes`.
  - Two doors for the same act compete: the hero sends to `/registration/new`, and "Empezar gratis" sends to `/planes#plan-gratis`.
- Fix:
  - At ≥980px make `.hero__intro` a two-column grid: h1, lead and `.hero__cta` on the left, `.hero__flow` on the right. Raise `max-width` to `var(--maxw)`. This puts the button inside 900px and fills the empty half.
  - Below 980px move `.hero__cta` directly after `.hero__lead` and before `.hero__flow`. That is a DOM reorder, not new copy.
  - In `#contacto` render `btn--primary` for the registration link and demote WhatsApp to `btn--ghost`.
- Suggested command: /impeccable layout

### [P2] Two visual systems plus decoration layers stand in for one authored world
- Why:
  - Light bands pair Cinzel caps, oxblood and a tracked eyebrow with a rule. Dark bands pair Inter, gold and `//` mono eyebrows (pretor.css:134-176).
  - Cinzel has no lowercase, yet it sets 60-word testimonials (`.testimonial__quote`, `.pullquote blockquote`), seven-line h2s (idx-d-01 "La dinámica de un estudio jurídico...") and every FAQ question. Long all-caps text reads slowly, worst on a phone in daylight.
  - Decoration stacks on top: a fixed film grain above everything (pretor.css:1175-1183, z-index 200), radial glows on every dark band (1185-1190), a zero-offset gold glow on the seam (1256-1261), and double frames from `outline-offset: 5px` around `.marco__grid` and `.empezar__list` (2130-2134, a frame around a frame).
  - Card copy is justified and hyphenated in about 34ch columns (2591-2599). Rivers are visible in the `#marco` and `#capacidades` tiles.
  - Non-interactive cards lift on hover (`.marco__card:hover`, `.cap__card:hover`, `.agent:hover`, pretor.css:1286-1289, 1318-1323, 736-738). That signals clickability that is not there.
- Fix:
  - Pick one display voice for both grounds. Keep Cinzel for the wordmark, the h1 and short maxims only. Set quotes and h2s longer than about 6 words in a face with lowercase.
  - Delete `body::after` grain, the section radial gradients, the seam glow, the subsection 27 outlines, the subsection 12.9 justification and the hover transforms on static cards.
  - Replace both eyebrow styles with nothing: the heading carries the section.
  - This is the groundwork for the black-and-white pass. It removes what color was holding together.
- Suggested command: /impeccable quieter

## Why the file and URL scans differ

1. Layout. The file scan has no layout engine. It cannot see padding that a child `.wrap` supplies (L107-112), `clamp()` padding on cards, or 1px-gap grids of padded cards. So cramped-padding drops from 47 to 1 on the landing page once rendered.
2. Rendered-only rules. radial-spotlight-glow, line-length, buried-raster and the `analytic-gradient+alpha` contrast path need a rendered surface. They appear only in URL scans.
3. Scan-time state. The URL scan caught the intro and the reveal fade: +10 alpha contrast hits, +1 buried-raster, +1 dark-glow and +1 gpt-thin-border-wide-shadow from `.prologo__cue svg` (L3019-3027). None of these exist under reduced motion (the intro exits at `pretor-intro.js` line 8 and the base CSS forces `.reveal` visible, L234-239).
4. Stylesheet-level rules. cream-palette, overused-font and the `#c8a35b` dark-glow fire from stylesheet text. The file scan resolves `--gold` to its first definition (`#c8a35b`, L49), not the override (`#cda455`, L1164). 5. low-contrast: the file scan reports both gold-on-stone lines. The URL scan reports one as a plain color pair and the rest through the alpha path.
